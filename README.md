### ⚡ About Me

Hi, I'm **Oscar**, a second-year **Electronics Degree Apprentice** based in England. I split my week between studying Electronic Engineering at **Sheffield Hallam University** and working on hardware at **Iceotope**.

I first got into programming during **lockdown**, teaching myself how to code from scratch. Building small scripts, games, and websites at home eventually led me to study electronics at university and land my apprenticeship.

Most of my week is focused on physical hardware now, so I have less free time for big software projects. When I do code, I focus on building practical tools I wish I had.

* 🔌 **Day-to-Day Focus:** Designing **PCBs** and **cable harnesses** in **Altium Designer**, with a side of **Embedded C** firmware.
* ☕ **Soft Spot for Code:** Even though my day job is hardware-focused, **Java** is still my all-time favorite programming language.
* 🖥️ **Side Projects:** Using my web design skills to build **Electron** desktop apps for everyday workflow gaps.
* 🔥 **Partner Pokémon:** Arcanine.
